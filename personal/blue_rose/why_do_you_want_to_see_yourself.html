WHY DO YOU WANT TO SEE YOURSELF?

It is 3:00AM. I just woke up, I rushed to switch the lights on. It has been a month or two since I moved here. I do not understand this place and I don't know why this awareness constantly forces me to crawl down the rabbit holes. These rabbit holes lack music of any form, they are cramped, suffocating, there is no track of time.

Today, my mother has come to visit me. She was tired from travel so she went to sleep early. I had difficulty going to sleep. Realisation is only pretension and in some time I was again there, in my dreams. I distinctly remember being in front of the dimly lit, very-old shop in the night, at the end of a cramped, dark street. The shop felt old and dirty in every sense: smell, sight and touch. Nobody visited the shop. It sold electrical parts that were not in use since a long time. He would sit facing one of the sidewalls, ignoring everything happening outside. This time I jotted courage to talk to talk to him,

"Do you know who I am?"

He replied with mild irritation, "No?"

I continued, "I moved here some time before. I frequently pass by your shop daily."

He started looking through old cardboard boxes for something. I asked him another question, "So, you seem to be living here for a long time, what do you think? Should I be aware of anything." He stopped and for the first time took an interest, started observing, hearing. After a long silence, he suddenly stretched to grab my arm and stared: straight through my eyes. I asked in irritation and anger,

"What are you doing?"

He replied, "You will not believe me."

I pushed him and rushed out of there, ran as fast as I could.

Suddenly, the street was all empty. I saw my mother had come outside, she seemed to be looking for me.

"Where were you, what are you doing?"

I shouted, "We have to leave this place right now. I always knew something was wrong. Let's go."

We started running away. Any sense of belief is temporary. I looked back: all darkness. My mother was silent. As we were running, I don't know why I started thinking: why she had come to visit me. I looked at her. She stared back: I knew she would. And everything turned black, there was this physical feeling of settling: like sand in water, like I will never wake up again, to experience any color, anything. There was something more in water: fear and satisfaction. And finally I did wake up. I could not believe anything anymore. I could not tell if this was the same darkness or a different one. I rushed to switch the lights on.



- Nimitt
