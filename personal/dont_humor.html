What exactly is humor?

What exactly is humor? In similar context, I read this quote somewhere, "Explaining a joke to someone is like dissecting a frog; they understand it better, but the frog dies in the process". To explain why I find it so accurate, let's talk about it. A joke is like a ride that makes the reader overturn their expectation. Avoiding any philosophoical undertones, something can only be overturned if it exists: the reader themselves need to be in the ride and build the "expectation". Only after this "expectation", some sort of punchline or twist in the end can work to create this "overturn". Over-explaining only curtails reader's anticipation and ability to enjoy the joke. So, I sincerely think that the only way to enjoy the joke is when you dissect the frog yourself.
